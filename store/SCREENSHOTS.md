# Screenshots and promo images

Chrome Web Store requires:

| Asset | Size | File in this repo |
|---|---|---|
| Extension icon | 128×128 PNG in the ZIP | `icons/icon128.png` |
| Small promo tile | 440×280 PNG or JPEG | `store/promo-small-440x280.png` |
| Screenshots | 1280×800 or 640×400, 1–5 images | **You capture these** |
| Marquee (optional) | 1400×560 | not included |

## How to capture screenshots

Do not use mockups or other people’s Reels as marketing art. Use a video you own (or a private test post).

1. Load the unpacked extension and open Instagram in a normal window (not Incognito unless the extension is allowed there).
2. Set the window so the capture is **1280×800** (or capture then crop to that size, square corners, no padding).
3. Take at least:
   - Reels or feed: seek bar visible at the bottom of the video
   - The same surface with the keep button visible (top-right of the video)
   - Optional: Stories with the seekable top strip
4. Upload those PNGs in the dashboard Graphic assets section.
5. Upload `promo-small-440x280.png` as the small tile. Do not add Instagram’s camera glyph or spectrum gradient to new art.

## Icon

The mark is a rose-pink tile with a white seek playhead. It does not use Instagram’s camera logo, “IG”, or brand gradient.
